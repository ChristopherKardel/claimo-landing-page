Delete your Claimo account

Version: 2.0
Last updated: 29 October 2026
Scope: Claimo accounts, associated app data and erasure requests
Public version: https://claimo-app.com/konto-loeschen.html

1. Request deletion without the app or a login

Email support@claimo-app.com stating that you want your account and associated personal data erased. Use the account email if possible. If you no longer have access, identify it and provide a reachable reply address. No particular wording, prior balance redemption or app installation is required to make the request. Handling is normally free.

An email link on this page only opens your mail application; you send the message yourself. You may alternatively use webmail. The website does not request a password or itself access your account. Do not send verification codes or unsolicited identity-document copies.

2. In-app self-service

The self-service route is Profile → Settings → “Permanently delete account”. Pending payouts or an account restriction may block that route. Support then considers your request independently of whether the individual matter is already resolved. Only specifically necessary data about an outstanding matter may remain longer, not the entire account wholesale. Admin accounts require a separate authorised review route.

3. Handling and response time

We securely identify the relevant account. Additional identification is requested only where reasonable doubts exist and only as necessary. We handle justified erasure without undue delay and normally tell you about the measures within one month of receipt. A legally permitted extension of up to two further months due to complexity or request volume is explained within the first month.

Our reply explains erased data and any remaining information, legal basis, purpose and duration or erasure criteria. Partial refusal is explained together with supervisory and judicial complaint options. The response period does not begin only after internal approval.

4. Effect on your account and balance

Permanent deletion ends account access. Progress not independently payable, such as XP and streaks, is lost. Uninstalling alone does not erase the account. Deletion does not automatically close your independent Google, Facebook, PayPal or game accounts.

Contact support to address remaining balance or outstanding payout claims. Voluntary deletion does not automatically extinguish legally enforceable claims already earned. A prior payout is not a condition for requesting erasure. Preserve any personal records you need before account access ends.

5. What is removed?

Erasure generally covers account/authentication records, profile, wallet and progress, transaction/payout links, referrals, associated partner information, settings, consent information, push tokens, inbox and linked security/analytics data. It follows statutory conditions and the limited exceptions below. Unnecessary account data is not merely deactivated.

Server-side deletion does not necessarily remove all local profile pictures or other files stored only on your device. Additionally clear app data in app/device settings. Protected storage may survive reinstallation, particularly on iOS.

6. Limited exceptions

  • Security records: Restricted or higher-risk accounts and payout/reversal cases may leave reduced records of protected identifier hashes, risk reasons and limited counters. They contain no names, account email or IP. Normal retention is 1,095 days after deletion; deletion of an under-16 account has a maximum of 180 days.
  • One-time device bonuses: A device-bonus record no longer linked to the account may normally remain for 1,095 days after deletion to prevent improper repeated grants.
  • Older partner receipts: Partner/transaction identifiers may remain without an internal account link for accounting and duplicate prevention. If still attributable, they remain subject to privacy duties; necessity and erasure criteria must be assessed separately. They must not be used for new advertising or active profiling.
  • Statutory documents: Necessary accounting vouchers may normally require eight years, business correspondence six and books ten (section 147 AO and, where applicable, section 257 HGB). Statutory time rules concern the necessary documents, not the whole account.
  • Outstanding matters and request evidence: Specifically necessary information for payout completion, justified legal claims and proof of erasure handling may remain restricted to those purposes until the reason and applicable periods expire.

We arrange necessary erasure with processors and notify other recipients where law requires. Independent partners handle their own records under their obligations. Time-limited backups are considered; restoration must not permanently undo effective erasures. Details: https://claimo-app.com/datenschutz-app.html#kontoloeschung.
