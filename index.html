Getting started

What is Claimo?

A rewards app for Android. You reach goals in games, app tasks or surveys from external providers and receive balance for confirmed goals, which you can redeem via PayPal or as a gift card.

Does Claimo cost anything?

No. Signing up and using the app are free, and a deposit is never required. External offers may include optional purchases, subscriptions or trials; you see their conditions on the offer before you start.

How old do I have to be?

Claimo is for adults only (18+). After signing up you confirm that you are of age; until then the features stay locked. Providers and countries may have additional conditions.

When and where can I get the app?

Claimo launches first on Android via Google Play. The store listing is not public yet; as soon as it is live, we will link it here.

Balance

How do I earn balance?

You reach goals in an offer. Once the provider confirms completion, the amount shown is credited – depending on the offer, with a delay. There are no fixed earnings and no guaranteed number of offers.

Why is part of my balance protected?

Credits from offers are protected for 48 hours from booking and are then released automatically, unless there is a reason to review them. Providers can also reverse credits later for legitimate reasons; the amount is then offset.

Is there a welcome bonus?

Currently yes: a one-time 5.00 in your wallet currency for eligible new accounts after completed onboarding and satisfied device-based requirements, once per device. The bonus is not immediately payable; the payout rules apply.

Which currency does my wallet use?

You choose USD, EUR, GBP or CHF once during setup. This choice cannot be changed afterwards, not even by switching the app language.

Payouts

What is the minimum payout?

Your first payout is possible from 20.00 of available balance in your wallet currency, further payouts generally from 5.00. Depending on the reward and region, the smallest available values may be higher.

Which payout methods are there?

PayPal and gift cards, for example from Amazon, Apple, Google Play, IKEA or Netflix. The selection depends on your country and wallet currency. Gift card codes arrive by email after the review.

How long does a payout take?

Every payout is reviewed manually and processed without undue delay. We do not promise payouts within minutes; you can see the status in the app.

Are there any fees?

Claimo does not charge a payout fee. Rewards in another currency are converted at the daily rate minus a current 0.5% deduction. PayPal or gift card providers may charge their own fees.

Account and help

A goal was not credited – what now?

Contact the provider’s support and/or support@claimo-app.com with the offer name, time period and evidence. We check for errors in our attribution and accounting.

How do I delete my account?

In the app or by email to support@claimo-app.com – even without the app or signing in. Cash out any available balance first. All steps are on the Delete account page.
