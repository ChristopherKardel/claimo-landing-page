1. Controller and contact

The controller is Timothy Kardel, trading as “Claimo”, Rilkestr. 8, 41541 Dormagen Zons, Germany. Privacy contact: support@claimo-app.com. No data protection officer is currently appointed. Provider information: https://claimo-app.com/impressum-app.html.

2. Scope

This policy covers accounts, authentication, rewards, partner offers, payouts, security, notifications and app support. For the website and its contact form, see https://claimo-app.com/datenschutz.html. Independent game, survey, authentication and payment services also have their own notices. Redirecting you to a service does not automatically make all its processing Claimo’s processing.

3. Overview

We process account and authentication details, age group, chosen wallet currency, balance and transactions, partner attribution, payout details, settings and security signals. A PayPal recipient address is payment/account identification data; we therefore do not claim to process no payment data at all. Creating a Claimo account does not require a credit card number or banking login details.

The app does not use GPS location or request contacts, camera or microphone content. A selected profile picture remains local. External offers may independently request additional information. Partners receive a separate random attribution identifier. External offer images and ayeT impression links can contact their servers when an offer list is displayed; see section 9.

4. Legal bases and device access

Article 6(1)(b) GDPR covers processing necessary for contracts and pre-contractual steps, such as authentication, balance management, offer attribution and payouts. Article 6(1)(c) covers legal evidence and retention duties. Article 6(1)(f) covers specifically necessary security, abuse prevention, legal claims and proportionate evaluation of existing operational data. Optional usage analytics, personalisation and promotional push notifications rely on Article 6(1)(a).

Storing or accessing information on your device is additionally subject to section 25 of Germany’s TDDDG. The exception for strictly necessary access, for example to provide a requested login, does not cover every technically possible access. Non-essential analytics, measurement or advertising access requires prior consent. A legitimate interest under the GDPR does not replace that consent. This also applies to locally queued analytics and tracking links used for those purposes.

You may withdraw consent for the future. This does not retrospectively affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. A privacy notice does not itself give consent or replace a necessary technical access restriction.

5. Age and minors

Claimo is intended for people aged 16 or over. After registration, you are asked for an age group. We store the confirmed group 16–17 or 18+, the confirmation time and the question version, not a full date of birth. Features remain restricted until required setup is complete. Contact support about an incorrect age classification; your rectification rights remain.

An under-16 declaration triggers a special server-side deletion process for the technical account. Necessary authentication and security steps may already have occurred before the age declaration. A necessary security record concerning suspicious activity may remain for no more than 180 days following such deletion.

External partner offers are not enabled for users aged 16 or 17 in the current app client. PayPal and Airbnb redemptions are restricted for this group. Being able to give data-protection consent does not replace any legal representative’s permission required for a user contract or external offer.

6. Authentication, account and email verification

For email login, Supabase processes your email, password as necessary for authentication, password hash, user identifier, session/refresh tokens and registration/login times. Profile details include display name, avatar letter, referral code, login method and any recorded contractual declarations. The display name may initially be generated from the part of your email before the @. Passwords are not stored as plain text in Claimo’s application database.

Google/Facebook authentication takes place with that provider. Claimo receives a provider identifier and email and, depending on permission, a name or profile-image address. The provider learns that you sign into Claimo. The Google or Meta entities identified in their notices are responsible for the authentication service. Claimo does not transmit your wallet history as part of this login.

In addition to authentication and password-reset emails, Claimo has its own short-lived email verification code. We process the recipient address, challenge identifier, protected code-verification value, delivery/expiry status, failed attempts and confirmation time. Resend delivers the message. The code currently lasts 15 minutes. Challenge records are pruned after seven days. An account-linked verification record remains until account deletion.

An unverified address may be corrected under the permitted conditions after a further password check. The password is used for authentication and is not passed to Resend or support. The bases are Article 6(1)(b) and, for abuse prevention, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Necessary verification is not advertising email.

7. Onboarding, settings and personalisation

Setup records your age group and fixed wallet currency. Gender and favourite game genres are optional. Language and settings are associated with your account. Before login, device language is read locally to suggest a language; IP geolocation is not used for this choice.

Personalised offers are off by default. With consent, genres are used for ranking and a provided gender is sent to ayeT-Studios for offer selection. Genres remain in Claimo’s database. Without enabled personalisation, these fields are not used to select personalised offers. You can turn personalisation off and remove stored personalisation information using the relevant deletion feature.

Our usage analytics has its own separate, default-off choice. Email status messages, personalisation and analytics are not the same consent. The final onboarding step records your choices, time and notice version. Settings can subsequently be changed in your profile. A later change to unrelated account details is not evidence of new consent.

8. Balance, progress and partner accounting

To provide the service, we store wallet currency, balance, transactions with amount, type and time, reserves, reversals, outstanding offsets, XP, levels, streaks and activity bonuses. This includes bonus eligibility, promotional windows, referrals, started offers, goals and transaction notifications. These records serve contractual performance and traceable accounting; optional analytics consent does not replace the contract as their basis.

For foreign-currency rewards, the original value, exchange rate, rounding and conversion evidence are recorded. The current Claimo rate includes a 0.5% deduction from the reference rate; the Terms of Use explain this. Exchange-rate requests themselves do not contain a user identifier or other personal account details.

9. Games, tasks and surveys

Each connected partner generally receives its own random identifier rather than your internal Claimo account ID. Claimo does not include your account email or name as standard entry parameters. Partners may separately receive information you provide directly on their pages.

When requesting the ayeT offer list, our server sends your partner identifier, IP address, device manufacturer/model, operating system/version, platform and app language. A provided gender is included only when personalisation is enabled. ayeT uses IP particularly for country and availability. The provider is ayeT-Studios GmbH, Germany. Privacy information: https://www.ayetstudios.com/privacy.

Offer artwork, icons and screenshots may load directly from servers specified in the offer, for example ayeT or i.ytimg.com. The image server then receives IP and technical connection details. These requests are distinct from ayeT impression links: requesting an impression link associates an offer display with a partner identifier for delivery/performance measurement. Measurement must not simply be treated as necessary image delivery; consent must precede device access wherever required. Turning off Claimo’s own server analytics does not automatically communicate a privacy choice to every partner.

Starting an offer uses a one-time Claimo link to the partner and then the offer page or store. Claimo records offer attribution and the start time for accounting and bonus eligibility. Games and their measurement providers may process installations, usage and goal completion under their own notices.

ayeT surveys may open in an embedded browser view. It can process browser data and cookies; Claimo does not grant it GPS access. Notik and CPX open in an integrated system browser view. Their providers receive IP, browser details, answers and, where applicable, cookies under their rules. Non-essential cookies or access require the relevant consent; opening an offer is not blanket agreement to unrestricted tracking.

Notik is the offerwall service provided under notik.me. Our server creates an entry link with a separate Notik identifier and app/publisher identifiers. Callbacks concern transaction, offer and sometimes goal identifiers, amounts, status and times; a callback may also contain a connection IP. Recipient categories are the offerwall operator and the independent offer partners you select. Official provider information: https://notik.me/privacy-policy and https://notik.me/terms-and-conditions. You can request information about actual recipients of your data through support@claimo-app.com.

CPX Research is provided by Make Opinion GmbH, Germany. Entry uses a separate partner identifier and security proof. Privacy information: https://www.cpx-research.com/main/en/privacy.php. CPX and survey partners process answers on their pages. These may include voluntarily requested sensitive answers; survey answers are not imported into Claimo profile fields for crediting.

TheoremReach Inc., USA, is technically connected as a survey service and may process data from earlier use or separately enabled entry points. A prepared service is not necessarily visible or active in every app build. Information: https://info.theoremreach.com/privacy-policy/. AdGem has a server-side callback integration; an active AdGem offer surface is not provided in this version.

Partners report confirmed or reversed activities directly to our server. Claimo stores the partner identifier, offer/transaction reference, amount, partner payment, status, time and technical validation values. Raw survey answers are not necessary to book rewards. Necessary attribution and credits rely on Article 6(1)(b), necessary abuse checks on Article 6(1)(f), and optional personalisation/measurement on the consent required for that processing. A partner’s independent responsibility does not remove Claimo’s responsibility for its own disclosure.

10. Paused advertising and diagnostics

Rewarded videos are paused in the current app version and server permissions. Onboarding does not enable them. Historical ad credits and necessary records may remain. Earlier test versions may have used Unity LevelPlay/Unity Ads or Google advertising services; their notices apply to their independent processing. Contact support@claimo-app.com to request information about earlier Claimo consents or associated data.

Sentry crash reporting and IPinfo network enrichment are prepared but are not currently enabled for normal processing. Before activation, data types, legal bases, providers and necessary consents must be reviewed and notices updated. Previously installed test versions may have different features.

11. Payouts and email delivery

For payout requests, we process user identifier, account email, display name, reward, amount, wallet currency, redemption country/marketplace, recipient address, request/review/payment times and status, and where needed rejection reasons, review history and notes. Authorised reviewers see account, transaction and security information necessary for their decision.

For PayPal payouts, PayPal receives the recipient address, amount and required transaction details. The provider for European PayPal accounts is particularly PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A., Luxembourg; further responsible entities depend on the recipient account’s terms.

Gift cards are procured from the provider or an authorised retailer. Purchasing a code does not normally require disclosure of your Claimo profile. The gift-card provider independently processes data when you redeem it. The full code is processed for delivery; Claimo payout records are normally intended to hold only a shortened reference. Legally required purchase/delivery evidence may be separately retained.

Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., USA) processes recipient address, subject, message content including any gift-card or verification code, and delivery information. This covers payout, status and email-verification messages. Information: https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy. The bases are Article 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR, with Article 6(1)(f) for necessary security review.

12. Security and device information

On Android the app uses an app/signature-specific Android ID; on iOS an IDFV provider identifier; otherwise a random installation identifier. It is stored in protected device storage and transmitted to the server. The designated Claimo security tables use secret-keyed hashes for device and IP signals. This is pseudonymisation, not automatically anonymisation. Hosting, authentication and partner providers may still process raw IP addresses in their own connection logs.

Play Integrity or App Attest may provide device/app evidence. Google or Apple process technical information; Claimo records results, reason codes, time and necessary hash/validation values instead of full raw attestation evidence. Depending on platform and verification mode, a signal is not absolute proof of a real individual.

For abuse prevention, necessary signals are assessed together with earnings, reversals, referrals, shared devices/recipient addresses and documented review decisions. Device-based bonus decisions are recorded for one-time rewards. Article 6(1)(f) GDPR applies; the interest is protecting the rewards service and other users from specific abuse. A security identifier must not be repurposed for advertising or analytics without a separate basis.

13. Push notifications and inbox

Push requires activation in the app and the required system permission. We process push token, user identifier, platform, timezone and category choices. Delivery uses Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple Push Notification Service. Quiet hours and frequency limits depend on category and timezone.

Factual credit/payout messages are distinct from advertising. Messages about new offers, returning to the app, open earning opportunities or expiring promotions may be advertising even if categorised as reminders. Promotional push requires prior consent. To stop all such messages in the current settings, disable both “Offers” and “Reminders”, or the push master switch. You may use the app without promotional push.

Reason, content, time, delivery status, failures and opening of a push may be logged. Comparison groups do not receive certain non-transactional messages so their effects can be evaluated. Consent to receive messages does not replace separate consent required for non-essential device access for analytics. Necessary delivery relies on Article 6(1)(b), advertising on Article 6(1)(a), and lawful limited operational evaluation on Article 6(1)(f). Important in-app events remain independently of push for no more than 90 days or 100 entries.

14. Own usage analytics and product tests

Optional usage analytics evaluates app opens, areas, onboarding steps, offer views/clicks and payout dialogs in our database. Events may include user attribution, time, session, platform, app version, offer, displayed reward and test variant. The designated events do not contain account email, names or raw IP.

The analytics switch controls acceptance of these client events into our own database. Analytics uses an installation identifier, sessions, event queue and sometimes locally stored test variants. Such non-essential local access requires prior consent just as the resulting evaluation does (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and section 25(1) TDDDG). Local queuing must not be used as a consent-free substitute when analytics is refused.

Technical information about the reviewed app version: events and test variants may currently be queued locally even when analytics is switched off. The switch prevents server acceptance of our own client events but does not yet provide a complete local access restriction. This information does not give consent for those device accesses; the technical discrepancy must be corrected before the relevant release is approved.

Necessary operational data arises separately from account creation, actual offer starts, credits, reversals and payout requests. Limited pseudonymous operational events, direct/referral acquisition, platform, app version and random product-test assignments support accounting, security and economic evaluation. Where Article 6(1)(f) is used, the right to object in section 23 applies. A test assignment does not automatically authorise additional device access without consent. Individual information may subsequently be converted into aggregated statistics.

15. Local storage and permissions

The app may store login session/tokens, language, security identifier, short-lived OAuth device proof, App Attest key identifier, push token, analytics identifier/session/queue/variants, image cache, your selected local profile picture and interaction hints. Necessary functional storage and optional analytics must be assessed separately. Historical test data may remain on the device until cleared.

Photo selection serves a local profile picture; uploading it to Claimo is not intended. System permissions concern notifications and, where applicable, the selected photo file. Account deletion alone does not guarantee removal of every local file. Local data can be removed through app/device settings. Depending on platform, protected storage can survive reinstallation; uninstalling, particularly on iOS, is not reliable evidence that all key/device information has been erased.

16. Support, sharing and reviews

For support we process sender address, message, attachments, time and history as necessary (Article 6(1)(b) or (f)). Your and our email providers are involved. The app’s email action opens your mail application and does not send without your action.

A copied referral code is written to the system clipboard. When sharing, the application you select processes the content under its own rules. Store reviews are handled by Google Play or the App Store. Claimo does not record your complete personal store review as its own profile field. Opening legal links visits the website, whose policy also applies.

17. Retention and account deletion

Storage is limited to necessary purposes and applicable obligations. The present data model particularly provides for:

  • Account, profile, wallet, transactions, linked payout requests and settings: generally for the life of the account, then deletion subject to the limited exceptions below.
  • Own raw analytics events, sessions and installation cohorts: normally 180 days; account-related test assignments until account deletion. Processed technical delivery queues are cleared sooner.
  • Device links without an outstanding bonus record: after 90 days without further use; attestation logs after 90 days. Short-lived signup reservations and request counters are separately cleared promptly; abuse request counters for no more than seven days.
  • Served offer data: normally 180 days; progress snapshots 30 days; started-offer attribution normally until account deletion.
  • Push delivery logs: 180 days; disabled push tokens normally 30 days after disabling. In-app inbox: no more than 90 days or 100 entries.
  • Email verification challenges: seven days; linked verification evidence until account deletion.
  • Support correspondence: until the matter is resolved and, where necessary, beyond that for specific evidence or statutory retention.

You may request account deletion in the app or without login through support@claimo-app.com. Instructions: https://claimo-app.com/konto-loeschen.html. Pending payouts or restricted accounts may block self-service deletion; support still considers your request. A payout is not a prerequisite for requesting deletion. Unnecessary account data must not be retained wholesale simply because one matter remains outstanding.

After deletion, restricted or higher-risk accounts and payout/reversal cases may leave a reduced security record: protected hashes of former user/recipient identifiers, a device hash where necessary, risk reasons/level, restriction status and reduced counters. It contains no account email, names or IP addresses and is normally removed 1,095 days after deletion; under-16 deletion has a maximum of 180 days. Orphaned device-bonus records are also normally removed 1,095 days after account deletion.

Certain older partner receipts may remain without an internal account link as evidence against duplicate crediting or for partner accounting. If they still contain attributable partner/transaction identifiers, they are not categorically described as anonymous. Their continuing necessity and erasure criteria must be assessed separately; they must not be used for new advertising or active profiling. Newer provider records may instead be deleted with the account.

Legally required accounting vouchers are generally retained for eight years, business correspondence for six, and books/relevant records for ten, where that classification applies (section 147 AO and, where applicable, section 257 HGB). The start of retention is governed by law, normally the relevant calendar year-end. This concerns required documents, not the entire account. The bases are Article 6(1)(c) and (f) and Article 17(3)(b) and (e) GDPR. Specific legal claims may justify limited additional retention.

Erasure with processors and, where applicable, notifying other recipients follow statutory requirements. Independent partners handle their own records under their obligations. Backups may contain data until overwritten in the normal cycle; effective erasures must be reapplied after restoration. When handling your request, we explain any specific remaining data, the reason and duration or erasure criteria.

You may request information about actual recipients of your data.

19. Processing outside the EEA

Services with US entities or other international infrastructure may process data outside the European Economic Area or allow access from there. A European database region does not automatically exclude such access. Transfers need an applicable basis under Articles 44 onward GDPR, for example an effective adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards including standard contractual clauses and necessary supplementary measures.

The EU-US Data Privacy Framework applies only to the particular certified US entity and covered processing, not to every US service. You may request information about actual recipients, countries and applicable safeguards and an accessible copy of safeguards from support@claimo-app.com. General consent to use the app does not replace a missing basis for regular transfers.

20. Automated checks and human review

Automated checks particularly concern age/product requirements, balance, offer attribution, duplicate transactions and device-based bonus rules. Risks may be assessed from security and accounting signals. Authorised reviewers make final decisions on submitted payout requests and permanent manual restrictions; technical entry requirements may prevent a request beforehand.

You may request human review, express your position and contest incorrect classifications, refused rewards or offsets. If a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects falls within Article 22 GDPR, its additional conditions and safeguards apply. Not every simple calculation or age check automatically falls within it. A risk score is not conclusive proof of fraud.

21. Security

Measures include TLS, restricted database access, controlled server functions, server-side accounting, verified partner callbacks, protected security hashes, role-restricted admin access and documented reviews. Necessary measures are adapted to risk. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Do not send passwords, unsolicited identity-document copies or unprotected gift-card codes to support.

22. Your rights

Subject to the statutory conditions, you have rights to access (Article 15), rectification (16), erasure (17), restriction (18), portability (20), objection (21) and withdrawal of consent (7(3) GDPR). Portability particularly concerns data you supplied that is processed automatically on a contractual or consent basis. Account deletion is not the only way to exercise these rights.

Contact support@claimo-app.com. Additional identification is requested only where reasonable doubts exist and only to the extent necessary. We normally respond within one month; any lawful extension is explained within that month. You may complain to a data protection authority, for example the North Rhine-Westphalia Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany, or another competent authority.

23. Objection and advertising opt-out

You may object to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) on grounds relating to your particular situation. It may then continue only under the statutory conditions, for example demonstrated overriding compelling grounds or legal claims. You may object to direct marketing at any time without a special reason; the data must then no longer be processed for that purpose.

For promotional push, consider both “Offers” and “Reminders”, or the master switch. You may also send your objection to support@claimo-app.com. Opting out of advertising does not remove balance claims already earned.

24. Required and optional information

An authentication address or supported provider login, age group and configured wallet currency are needed for the intended account functions; a suitable recipient address is needed to deliver a payout. A technical account may exist before setup is complete. Affected features cannot be provided without the required details.

Gender, genres, referral code, profile picture and optional consents are voluntary. Refusal of optional analytics or advertising is not a blanket condition for retaining an account or earned claims. Some personalised offers cannot be provided without information necessary for them.

This policy is updated when processing or law changes. Its version identifies the information provided and is not automatic new consent. We give appropriate notice of material changes; new processing requiring consent needs prior permission. Changes to contracts are separately governed by the Terms of Use. The current published policy is accessible through the URL above and the app’s legal links.
